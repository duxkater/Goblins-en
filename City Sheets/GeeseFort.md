# Size : 6

# Impressions :

- A peaceful village with timber-and-thatch houses
- Crumbling walls, smothered in brambles
- A rusted alarm bell that hasn’t rung in years
- An old tree in the middle of the square; its gnarled roots are heaving up the cobblestones.
- A small, bustling market square where several farmers are selling their produce.
- Children have drawn on the walls of an abandoned house.
- Lavender fields bordered by low white stone walls.
# Locations
## The village's central square

A small, round, cobblestone square featuring a centuries-old tree at its center, its branches draped in string lights.

A small market is held here, where local farmers come to sell their produce.

- **Paint the scene:** What signs indicate that this is the busiest spot in the village, even at night?
## The perimeter wall

An old stone wall, showing signs of wear and tear in places. The white stone blocks are cracking under the weight of brambles and wild overgrowth. Wooden watchtowers stand along it, each manned by sentries.

- **Paint the scene:** This wall once withstood an assault by an invading army. What traces of that attack remain visible?
## The Alarm Bell

Perched atop a 2-meter-tall wooden structure, the bell is cracked. A frayed rope dangles to the ground, swaying listlessly in the breeze.

- **Paint the scene:** What measures have the locals taken to keep the wooden structure, which threatens to collapse, standing?
## The Temple

A stone building adorned with purple hangings. Incense smoke drifts from the windows. It is surrounded by massive, carved columns draped in hundreds of small copper lanterns.

- **Paint the scene:** Travelers are invited to carve a small image onto the exterior pillars, depicting their adventures, thereby turning the temple into a colossal archive of the world's history. What carving can be seen that reveals the human disdain for goblins?
## ## **The Tavern**

A tavern stands at the edge of town. Its walls are made of planks painted in a riot of colors. Laughter and singing spill out from within. A stable sits out front.

Inside, long solid-wood tables—surrounded by dozens of benches and stools—are laden with simple yet well-prepared fare: soups, roasted vegetables, grilled sausages, cheeses... A large fireplace holds a battered cast-iron cauldron, bubbling away.

- **Paint the scene:** Various groups are scattered around the room, chatting or laughing. What signs reveal that some of these humans have come from very far away?
# Clues :

- A temple member exits through a side door, hastily hiding the key under a stone.
- The portcullis appears to be made of rotted wood.
- The perimeter wall is neglected in places; entire sections have collapsed.
- A local woman hides an old grimoire behind her fireplace hearth (unlocks the **Forbidden Tome** perk).
- The village geese are unnatural: they keep watch and report what they see (unlocks the **Trained Geese** perk).
- A strange purple glow emanates from the tavern cellar.
- Villagers are afraid to utter certain words, as if they were cursed.
- Some villagers seem to enter and leave the temple very regularly (unlocks **Truth Serum**).
- An old blind woman asks passersby for money.
- A group of children playing against the temple wall are chased off by a monk.
- Several bottles of alcohol hidden in the brambles near a watchtower.
- A gold coin lying in the mud in an unusual spot.
- Wagon wheel tracks coming from the north.
- A torn book page covered in strange symbols.
- An icy wind blows constantly through one alleyway, regardless of the season.
- Signs of fire damage around the ruins of an old house.
- A very old leather bag abandoned against a wall, covered in moss and dust.
## Rewards:

- **Forbidden Tome**: Occasionally allows you to summon a small earth golem. Be careful not to miss a single word.
- **Truth Serum**: A vial of golden liquid. Once per scouting phase, force an NPC who drinks it to reveal a clue.
- **Trained Geese**: The goblins capture a few geese from the village. Once per session, they can serve as a distraction by following simple commands.
