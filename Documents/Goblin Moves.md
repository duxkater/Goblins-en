## Sorcerer

You found this shiny stone and decided to embed it in your forehead. Since then, you have felt ancient magic flowing through you. Choose an element from: water, fire, lightning, acid, or rust—or decide on an element with the GM. When you attempt to use magic aligned with your element, roll 2d6 + Smash.

On a 10+, everything goes as planned.

On a 7–9, choose 1:

- You are at odds with your element; you must perform a ritual during the next short phase.
- The god of your element seems displeased; you must appease them.
- Your spell works a little too well, causing a catastrophe. Describe it.
- Your magical talent attracts unwanted attention.
## Sharp

You’ve always had a bit more savvy than your peers. You can read and write the human tongue as well as one other language of your choice. Additionally, when helping to answer the question "How do we attack this place?", you can ask the GM for a clue that wasn't discovered during the scouting phase. If all clues have already been found, choose one clue; it can be used twice.
## Burly

You are strong—very strong. In fact, you are far more muscular than your clanmates. You gain +1 to Smash. You can also use human weapons and armor.

Secret – You have a natural knack for going unnoticed, slipping into the shadows, and finding passages where others see none. When you attempt to infiltrate a location, roll 2d6 + Evade.

On a 10+, ask 2 questions

On a 7–9, ask 1 question

- How can I reach my goal without being spotted?
- What should I watch out for here?
- What could go wrong?
- Where are the escape routes in case things go south?
## Strategist

Others listen when you speak—that’s just how it is. You aren’t necessarily stronger or smarter, but your opinion carries more weight than anyone else's. When you give an order to a goblin NPC, they obey you. Once per Assault Phase, you can modify a Clue to use it in a different way.
## Cook

You know how to transform even the most repulsive ingredients into something exquisite. In fact, no one dares ask what’s in your cooking pot anymore. Once per session, describe the meal you are preparing; it will make you and your clan stronger and more alert. Once per session, any goblin PC who has eaten your food can reroll a failed check.
## Trainer

Others don’t understand your bond with animals. Why eat a horse when it can be useful in so many other ways? Choose an animal. Once per session, you can choose to perform an action with your animal’s help. Describe how it assists you; the check automatically succeeds.
## Seer

You have had visions of the beyond since birth. Spirits speak to you, and you consult them when needed. The first time you discover a clue during a session, the spirits immediately point out a second one nearby.
## Death-Spitter

Your breath is a unique blend of rotting fungi and toxic herbs. Once per session, you can exhale a cloud of unbearable miasma that forces a target to flee, faint, or vomit.
