## Eternal Hunger

Clan members suffer from constant hunger.
During every rest phase, the clan consumes one extra unit of food.
## Cursed Blood

The first time a goblin bleeds during a scouting or raid phase, a foul stench emanates from them, spreading for about ten meters and persisting until the next victory phase.
## Haunted by Iron

Spirits sometimes manifest when a clan goblin touches a metal object. The larger the object, the more powerful and malevolent the spirit.
## Shadow of the Great Eater

The Great Eater pursues the goblins even into their nightmares. Sometimes, they think they catch a glimpse of it out of the corner of their eye and are paralyzed with fear.
## Blood of the Serpent-Folk

Stealing the Serpent Lord’s chalice was a bad idea. Sometimes, when a goblin bleeds, they are detected by the serpent-folk, who send a handful of vipers to investigate.
## Ashes of the Ancient Fire

Sometimes, when a goblin touches a wooden object, it instantly bursts into flames.
