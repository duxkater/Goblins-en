GOBLINS is a game where you play a band of goblins, belonging to the same clan, trying to survive and prosperate by raiding villages and cities built by humans, dwarfs or elves. Smart, chaotic and often underestimated, you stealthily infiltrate cities, steal every shiny thing you can and sow panic before disappearing in the night. This way of life vows the clan to a grim future, because of the people you infuriate.
## Your clan

The clan is more than just thirty goblins squabbling after a meager meal by the fireside; it is a family. It is more than a band of raiders; it is a haven of camaraderie in a world where no one likes greenskins. This life of raiding and pillaging is forced upon you, and the only respite offered by this cruel world, which seems meant for others, is an evening spent playing knucklebones and drinking mushroom cider with your comrades.

Without the clan, you are nothing but a wretched goblin at the mercy of the first guard who comes along.
## Player Goblins

The term “player goblins” refers to the characters portrayed by the people around the table (excluding the GM).  
They are the clan’s elite members—smarter, stronger, or more attuned to the magical winds. They are the ones sent on reconnaissance, and they are the ones who lead the charge.  
They are recognized within the clan.
## The Factions

The locations subjected to raids by the player-goblins and their clan belong to factions, which will be created at the start of the campaign. In addition to possessing villages, crops, and leaders, these factions also maintain relationships with one another; relationships that can be hostile.
## Towns

Let’s be clear: we are talking here about a settlement; whether inhabited by humans, elves, or more rarely dwarves; regardless of its size.

As long as there are riches to plunder, food to steal, and gear to scavenge, even the filthiest hamlet counts as a town. A target for the clan. Be it the village of GeeseFort or the sun-city of Saro-thaan.
## How the game works

The game is based on a conversation between the players, who work together to build the story of their raids and their clan's survival. One person takes on the role of Game Master (GM), responsible for bringing the world to life, presenting challenges, and reacting to the goblins' actions.
### What you need to play:

- Six-sided dice
- One character sheet per player
- The clan sheet
- Town sheets corresponding to the locations being raided

The game follows a cycle structured into five phases, each played in order.

1. Rest Phase – The clan recovers after a raid.
2. Scouting Phase – Goblins infiltrate the city to gather intel.
3. Planning Phase – They plan the attack by leveraging their findings.
4. Raid Phase – The clan springs into action, sowing chaos and destruction.
5. Victory Phase – The consequences of the raid on the clan and the world are assessed.

Each cycle represents a raid on a city.
These phases are explained in detail in the "Game Phases" section.
