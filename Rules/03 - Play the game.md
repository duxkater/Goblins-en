# The Conversation

The game takes the form of a conversation among everyone at the table. One person takes on the role of the GM; their job is to frame the conversation and describe the world the goblins inhabit—as well as how that world reacts to the goblins' actions.

Players describe how their goblins act, as well as what they are thinking or feeling.
# Stats

To represent your goblin's strengths and weaknesses, the game uses stats ranging from -3 to +3. These are assigned during character creation and can change over time.

- **Smash:** when your goblin fights, or uses strength to push, pull, hold back, etc.
- **Dash:** when your goblin needs to run, flee, or think fast.
- **Evade:** when your goblin needs to sneak, spy, hide something, etc.
- **Observe:** when your goblin searches a location, watches someone, looks for something, etc.
- **Influence:** when your goblin tries to intimidate, lies, attempts to persuade, etc.
# Advancement

Your goblin evolves over the course of raids, becoming increasingly experienced. To represent this, whenever you fail an action roll, you can mark 1 XP. Once you have 6 XP, you can mark an advancement and reset your XP total to zero.
When you have marked all your advancements, it is time for your goblin to retire. You can create a new one or choose another goblin from the clan.
# Conditions

Conditions represent your goblin's physical state. They usually arise during combat or a particularly dangerous action.
When your goblin acquires a condition, mark it next to one of your attributes that does not yet have one. Choose a very brief description for the condition (e.g., *gash, blow to the head, fright*).

All rolls involving that attribute are made with disadvantage until the condition is removed.

If all of your goblin's attributes are marked with a condition, they are too exhausted to continue fighting and must withdraw until the next short phase (consultation phase or victory phase).

# Rolling the Dice

When an action calls for it, the GM asks you to roll the dice. Rolls always involve two six-sided dice (2d6), to which you generally add one of your goblin’s stats.

For example, rolling 2d6 + *Charge* means adding the result of the two dice to your *Charge* stat.

The result tells you your degree of success:

- **On a 10 or higher**, it’s a success: you achieve your goal without a downside.
- **Between 7 and 9**, it’s a partial success: you achieve your goal, but something doesn't go as planned—there is a cost to pay or a difficult choice to make.
- **6 or lower**, it’s a failure. The GM describes how things go wrong.
# Actions

When a player’s description triggers an action, the conversation pauses while the group follows the instructions for that action. It’s like a snippet of rules stepping into the conversation, but only when the fiction triggers it. Once the action is resolved, the conversation resumes.
## Field Action

When a goblin player attempts something risky or with an uncertain outcome, they roll using the appropriate stat.

- On a 10+: The action succeeds. The goblin player describes what happens.
- On a 7–9: The action succeeds, but there is a complication. The GM describes what happens.
## Scouting Action

When a goblin player searches the area for a clue during the scouting phase, they roll using the appropriate stat.

- On a 10+: They find a clue. The GM describes it to them.
- On a 7–9: They find a clue, but the GM also describes a complication.
## Diversion Action

When a goblin player attempts to shake off pursuers after being spotted during the scouting phase, they roll +Avoid.

- On a 7+: Their pursuers lose their trail.
- On a 10+: They find a clue.
## Scavenge Move

When a player-goblin searches a location for loot during the raid phase, roll +Spot.

- On a 10+: They find something interesting. The GM describes the find. Add 3 to the clan's wealth tracker.
- On a 7–9: They find something of little value. The GM describes the find. Add 1 to the clan's wealth tracker. Additionally, choose 1:
	- cost
	- danger
	- suspicion
## Inventory Move

When a player-goblin pulls an item from their pack to use for an action, spend 1 clan supply and announce the item to the group. Then, perform the next action with advantage.
