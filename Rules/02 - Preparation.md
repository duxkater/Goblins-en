Before you start playing, you need to complete a few steps to establish a coherent and engaging setting; specifically, the region where your game will take place, as well as the clan and the goblins that make it up.
## Creating the Region

Take a large sheet of paper and take turns placing geographical features such as towns, rivers, coastlines, and mountains; wherever they seem to make sense. Let your imagination run wild.  
The map doesn’t need to be highly detailed; a simple sketch will suffice.  
Once you are satisfied with the result, move on to the next step.

Each player, including the GM, creates a neighboring faction, outlines its territory on the map, gives it a name, and writes a brief description. Who leads it? What is its local specialty? How does it get along with neighboring factions?  
  
For instance, here is an example of a faction:  
*The Order of Saro-Thaan*
- *A human people dwelling on the slopes of the Twin Mountains.*
- *Deeply mystical, they are led by Kasheen, a prophetess.*
- *They are renowned for their luxury goods: silk, incense, amber...*
- *Though not particularly open to the outside world, they are a prime destination for merchants who sometimes travel from afar to acquire their rare wares.* 
  
For each faction, the GM creates a corresponding 6-segment clock, starting at zero. This clock represents the level of attention the faction is paying to the goblin clan. The more the clock fills up, the more seriously the faction takes the goblin threat and the more resources it deploys to eliminate it.  
Once everyone has created a faction, it is time to create the clan.

## Populating the Clan

Goblins aren't just faceless looters; they belong to a clan, a dysfunctional yet endearing family. To reinforce this bond, each PC is linked to two important goblins who matter to them. Creating a goblin companion involves the following steps:

- Choose a name. If you’re short on inspiration, here are a few examples: *Grik-Grik, Sludge, Grime-Finger, Mighty Momo, Zlab-Zlab, Gut-Burn, Patch-Eye, Grok-Chew, Zog-the-Wise.*

- Choose an archetype from the following:
	- The Protégé: a younger or weaker goblin who relies on them.
		*"He’s small, he’s weak, and he counts on you. One day, he might just become a real goblin... if he survives that long."*

	- The Comrade: a goblin of the same age with whom they share a strong bond (rivalry, friendship, admiration...).
		*"You grew up together, sharing both blows and laughter. You are bound together... one way or another."*

	- The Elder: a wise goblin or veteran who offers advice, whether sound or nonsense.
		*"He’s seen things. He claims to know secrets. He whispers truths... or absurdities."*

These goblins aren't just for show; they serve a mechanical purpose:

- **The Protégé:** Once per session, when an event threatens your protégé (a trap, a fight, a fire...), you can choose to save them automatically by describing how. In return, they reveal a clue (during the scouting phase) or some loot (during the raid phase).

- **The Comrade:** Once per session, if you describe how your comrade assists you with an action, you roll with advantage.

- **The Elder:** Before an assault or infiltration, the Elder can give you a "sign" or a "piece of advice" that only you can hear. Once per session, if you follow this advice, you roll with advantage on a related action. But beware : sometimes the Elders are senile or mad...
# Create Your Goblins

Each player-goblin is an elite member of the clan and an essential part of its workings. They are the ones sent out to scout and the ones on the front lines. Furthermore, they each possess a talent that makes them unique.

- **Choose a name**
- **Allocate your stats:** Assign each of these scores to one of your attributes: -1, 0, 0, 1, 2
- **Choose a goblin action** (see the goblin actions sheet). No two goblins in the group may have the same talent.
- **Choose a lucky charm:** a small object that brings you good fortune (e.g., a tooth from your favorite old worg, a clay pipe stolen from Grom-the-Mad...). Where did you get it? What does it do for you? Once per raid phase, you can describe how the charm helps you perform an action and roll with Advantage on that check.
- Your goblin has a major flaw. **What is it?** If this flaw complicates a goblin action, you roll with Disadvantage.

**Once these steps are complete, you are ready to play.**
